Terms and privacy

Plain words on how buying works and what this site records. Last updated 18 November 2026.

What you are buying

Each Stack Method book is a PDF file. Nothing is posted to you. You can read it on a phone, tablet or computer, or print it for yourself.

Paying and getting your book

The buy buttons take you to Payhip, which runs the checkout, takes the payment and sends you the download. Your payment details go to Payhip and its payment providers, not to us. Payhip’s own terms and privacy policy apply to the checkout.

Prices and discounts

Prices are in pounds sterling. When a sale is on, the price you pay is the one shown at the Payhip checkout. If a discount needs a code, type the code at the checkout before you pay.

Refunds

You get the file as soon as you pay, so we do not refund a change of mind. If your file is faulty, incomplete or will not open, tell us and we will send a working copy or refund you. Nothing here takes away rights you have by law.

What you can do with the file

  • Use it yourself, on as many of your own devices as you like, and print it for your own use.
  • Please do not share, resell, upload or copy it for other people. Each copy may be marked with the buyer’s details.
  • Teachers and tutors who want to use a book with a class: get in touch first.

What the books can and cannot do

The books teach the words and give you the practice. How far you get depends on doing the chapters and saying them out loud, so we cannot promise a particular result.

What this website records

  • No cookies and no visitor IDs.
  • We count page views and button taps without knowing who you are. Each count stores the time, which button, the site you came from, and whether you were on a phone, tablet or computer.
  • The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and its settings from our database host, Supabase. Like any website request, those see your IP address.
